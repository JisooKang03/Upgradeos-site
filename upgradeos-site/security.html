Security

Privacy-first,
secure by design.

Security isn't a feature we add later. It's the foundation everything else is built on.

Encryption

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, so financial information stays protected end to end.

Secure authentication

Account access is protected with modern authentication standards, not shared passwords sitting in a spreadsheet.

Data ownership

Your financial data belongs to you. UpgradeOS does not sell personal data to third parties.

Privacy-first architecture

Products are designed to collect only what's needed to deliver the feature — nothing more.

Important

No financial advice

UpgradeOS Ledger organizes and summarizes your financial information. It does not provide licensed financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Always consult a qualified professional for decisions specific to your situation.

Our security philosophy

We believe trust is earned through transparency, not marketing language. As UpgradeOS grows — from Ledger into accounting services, AI tools, and beyond — every product will be held to the same standard: clear disclosures, minimal data collection, and security practices that are explained plainly rather than buried in fine print.
