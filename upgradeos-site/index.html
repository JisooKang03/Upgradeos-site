Frequently asked

Is Ledger free during beta?

Yes — UpgradeOS Ledger is free to use while in beta. Pricing details for the full release are on the product page.

Is UpgradeOS a bank?

No. UpgradeOS builds software. Ledger connects to your existing accounts to organize information — it never holds your money.

Does UpgradeOS give financial advice?

No. Our products provide organization and insight, not licensed financial advice. See our Security page for details.

What's next after Ledger?

Accounting services, a business platform, original games, and AI productivity tools. See the full roadmap.
