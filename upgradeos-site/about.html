Our Story

Why UpgradeOS
exists.

UpgradeOS started with a simple frustration.

Managing money required spreadsheets, banking apps, budgeting apps, notes, and manual tracking. Instead of making better financial decisions, people spent their time maintaining systems.

UpgradeOS was created to eliminate that complexity. Every product we build follows one philosophy:

  • 01Remove friction
  • 02Increase clarity
  • 03Give people better control over their lives
Founder & CEO

John Kang

CPA Candidate · Professional Accountant

Before UpgradeOS, John spent his career inside the numbers — reconciling accounts, preparing statements, and watching good businesses and good people get buried under the same manual busywork. As a CPA candidate, he saw the problem from both sides: individuals drowning in spreadsheets at home, and businesses drowning in the same spreadsheets at work.

Ledger was built to answer a question John kept asking in his own accounting practice: why should clarity require this much manual effort? He set out to build the tool he wished existed — one that organizes the numbers automatically, so people can spend their time making decisions instead of maintaining systems.

Ledger is the first product. It won't be the last. John's plan is for UpgradeOS to grow into a full technology company — one that builds software, offers accounting services, and even develops games — all guided by the same philosophy: remove friction, increase clarity, and give people better control over their lives.
